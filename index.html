MineHeart PvP Guides

Everything you need to know about tier testing, points, and climbing the leaderboard.

Guide 01

How to Get Tier Tested on MineHeart

Connect to play.mineheart.net and join our 1k-member Discord server. Use /tiertest in the lobby to select your gamemode and region. Once matched with a tester, you duel in a standardized arena kit to earn your placement (Tier 5 up to Tier 1).

Guide 02

Minecraft PvP Tiers Explained: What HT1 to LT5 Mean

Every gamemode uses 5 main tiers (Tier 1 being the highest, Tier 5 being entry competitive). Each tier is split into High Tier (HT) and Low Tier (LT) subdivisions, with R- indicating a retired legacy tier.

Guide 03

MineHeart Points and Titles: How Overall Works

Your Overall score sums your points across all 8 gamemodes based on the active point weights configured in the Admin Panel. Reaching higher totals unlocks titles like Combat Ace, Combat Master, and Combat Grandmaster.
